Blades of Ages

Delete your account

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Delete it in the game

  1. Open Blades of Ages and tap the Settings button on the home screen.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete account.
  3. Read the warning and tap Yes, delete.

An internet connection is required. Once deletion is complete, the game starts over.

What is deleted

  • Your account, your random ID and your sign-in sessions
  • Your maps, stars, gold, diamonds (including purchased ones, which are not refunded), gear and inventory
  • Your hero and look choices, upgrades, loadouts, lives and run tickets
  • Your battle recordings and daily trial results
  • The progress on your device (the game resets)

Deletion is permanent and takes effect immediately; the data is removed from the encrypted backups within 30 days. For cheating and refund checks, diamond transactions and cheating flags are kept for up to 12 months with the link to your account removed, and purchase and refund records (product, transaction ID, date) are kept as the record of the sale. A record of the deletion itself, with only a date and a random request number, is kept for 24 months. Your store receipts stay in your Apple account.

If you made a purchase, your customer record at RevenueCat, the service that verifies our purchases, is not deleted automatically when you delete your account. If you want it deleted, write to {{CONTACT}} and we will have it deleted.

If you cannot open the game

If you have removed the game from your device, your account remains on the server. To have it deleted, send an email with the subject “Delete my account” to {{CONTACT}}. Because we keep no name or email, we may ask for more information to find your account and confirm that the request comes from you (for example, if you made a purchase, the order number from your Apple receipt). Once we have verified the request, we will carry it out within 30 days and let you know by email.
