Common questions

Does the game show ads?

There are no forced ads. This version of the game has no ads at all.

Can I play without internet?

Yes, for a short while. The game lets you fight offline using the run tickets stored on your device; the results are verified on the server when you are back online and the rewards arrive then. When the tickets run out, offline battles give no rewards. The daily trial and the store need a connection.

I did not get the reward for a battle

The server grants rewards after replaying and verifying the battle's recording. On a slow connection this can take a few minutes. Keep the game open while you are online; a result that cannot be verified does not count.

The diamonds I bought have not arrived

Diamonds are added to your account once Apple confirms the purchase; on a slow connection this can take a few minutes. If the game says the purchase is awaiting approval (for example when Ask to Buy is on), they are added once it is approved. If they still have not arrived, write to us with the order number from your Apple receipt.

My diamond balance is below zero

When Apple refunds a purchase, the diamonds it granted are deducted from your balance. If you had already spent them, the balance goes below zero and new diamonds pay that off first. This never creates a debt you have to pay.

My progress is not on my new phone

For now your account is tied to the device it was created on; this version has no way to move progress to another device.

Game language

Change it in Settings → Language. The new language appears once you close and reopen the game.

I want a refund

Apple takes the payment for in-app purchases; you can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
