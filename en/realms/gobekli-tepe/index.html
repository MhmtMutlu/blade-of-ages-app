Realm of Göbekli Tepe: Hill of Standing Stones

The realm of Göbekli Tepe covers maps 1–10, and its map is called Hill of Standing Stones. Totem stones strengthen nearby enemies; swords can break them. Here you fight Stone Boar, Scorpion Spirit and Fox Shade; the elite is Totem Bearer and the boss is Mother Vulture.

Exhibit note

In myth and history

Göbekli Tepe is an archaeological site near Şanlıurfa in southeastern Türkiye, dating to the Pre-Pottery Neolithic. The German Archaeological Institute (DAI), which leads the excavation, dates it to roughly 9500 to 8000 BCE. Its best-known features are large round-to-oval buildings with T-shaped limestone pillars inside them; the two central pillars stand about 5.5 m tall. Members of the excavation team read the pillars as abstract human figures, because they carry reliefs of arms, hands, belts and animal-skin loincloths. Most of the carved animals are wild: foxes, birds and snakes are the most common, and boars predominate in one enclosure.

Pillar 43 in Enclosure D is dominated by a large vulture that lifts its left wing while its right wing points towards a nearby sphere or disc. The researchers suggest it may be a narrative scene, but its meaning is not settled. The purpose of the site is also debated. For its first two decades of research it was read mainly as a ritual place; more recent excavations have found evidence of domestic activity and indications of permanent settlement, and the DAI now describes it as a settlement with a strong ritual component. The site predates writing by thousands of years, so no myth text is attached to it.

Where the game departs from the myth

  • "Mother Vulture" (Akbaba Ana) is the game's own invention, inspired by the vulture relief on Pillar 43. The relief shows a vulture whose meaning is not settled; presenting it as a mother figure or a boss is the game's interpretation.
  • The game's "totem stones" that strengthen enemies are a game mechanic. The excavators interpret the T-shaped pillars as abstract human figures.
  • Enemies such as the Stone Boar and the Fox Shadow draw on the boars and foxes in the reliefs; in the reliefs they are carved animals, not spirits or shadows.

This section describes the myth and the history themselves; the sections below are the game's interpretation. Sources are at the end of the page.

In the game: enemies

Each enemy teaches you something. At the end of the realm the elite tests it at its hardest.

  • Stone Boar

    Charger

    Paws the ground for 0.6 s and charges in a straight line. It is stunned when it hits a wall.

    Teaches: reading attack warnings

  • Scorpion Spirit

    Poisonous

    Draws its tail back, stabs poison into the ground and leaves a short-lived poison pool.

    Teaches: avoiding ground damage

  • Fox Shade

    Teleporter

    Appears and vanishes; it shows up behind the player and bites. It is fragile the moment it becomes visible.

    Teaches: that the swords guard all around you

  • Elite

    Totem Bearer

    Elite / mini boss

    The mini boss of the first session (3:30). The totem it carries strengthens the enemies around it.

    Teaches: choosing priority targets

The realm's kit

The realm's own weapon, armour set and myth power.

  • Realm weapon

    Obsidian Blades

    A short, fast-spinning orbit; low damage, very frequent hits.

  • Armour set

    Totem Hide

    2 pieces: +10% health. 4 pieces: Defeating an enemy restores health.

  • Myth power

    Standing Stones

    4 stone pillars rise; a shock wave at once, then they push back approaching enemies for the duration.
