Realm of Sumer: Cedar Forest of Uruk

The realm of Sumer covers maps 11–20, and its map is called Cedar Forest of Uruk. Roots set traps from the ground. Here you fight Winged Djinn, Clay Golem and Scorpion Man; the elite is Bull of Heaven and the boss is Humbaba.

Age
I · First Age
Maps
Maps 11–20
Mythology
Mesopotamia · The Epic of Gilgamesh
Map rule
Roots set traps from the ground.

In the game: enemies

Each enemy teaches you something. At the end of the realm the elite tests it at its hardest.

  • Winged Djinn

    Flying

    Glides through the air in groups and drops fireballs. It passes over obstacles.

    Teaches: aiming on the move

  • Clay Golem

    Tank

    Slow and very tough. When the cuneiform on its body glows, it sends out a wave of punches around it.

    Teaches: choosing priorities

  • Scorpion Man

    Guardian

    In the epic, the guardian of the mountain's gate. It jabs with a spear and stings from behind with its tail.

    Teaches: threats from two sides

  • Elite

    Bull of Heaven

    Elite

    The bull Ishtar sends in tablet VI of the Epic of Gilgamesh. It paws the ground for 0.8 s (red line) and charges in a straight line; at the end of the charge it bellows and 3 red pit rings open around it (they last 4 s). After a charge it is stunned for 1.2 s. The lapis stone between its horns is its weak point (×2 damage).

    Teaches: punishing after a charge

Boss · map 20

Humbaba

The guardian of the Cedar Forest in the Epic of Gilgamesh.

The realm's kit

The realm's own weapon, armour set and myth power.

  • Realm weapon

    Bronze Sickle Sword

    A bronze sickle sweeps a full circle around the hero; it pulls the enemies it cuts toward the ring of swords.

  • Armour set

    Cedar Guardian

    2 pieces: Hero mastery 15% cheaper. 4 pieces: Stand still for 1 s for a root field: enemies inside are 30% slower.

  • Myth power

    Cedar Shadow

    Temporary shadow swords join the orbit; they briefly exceed the 8-sword limit.
