Realm of Egypt: Hourglass Desert

The realm of Egypt covers maps 21–30, and its map is called Hourglass Desert. A sandstorm narrows your sight every 30 seconds. Enemies here: Mummy, Scarab Swarm and Jackal Guard. Elite: Sphinx Warden. Boss: Apep, Serpent of Chaos.

Exhibit note

In myth and history

Apep (Greek Apophis) is the giant serpent who is the great enemy of the sun god Ra in ancient Egyptian religion. He lies outside the created world and stands for chaos. According to Emory University's Michael C. Carlos Museum, if Apep defeated the sun god during his nightly journey through the underworld, the sun would not rise and creation would collapse; Ra and his defenders attack the serpent with sharp knives. The Global Egyptian Museum glossary adds that Apep was linked with the darkness of eclipses and clouds, and that he tries to halt the sun's boat, for example by drinking up the water so that it runs aground.

The struggle never ends for good: the sun god has to fight again each time he rises. Rituals were therefore performed against Apep. The Bremner-Rhind papyrus, now in the British Museum, contains spells against him that were meant to be recited daily in the temple of Amun at Karnak. The realm's elite draws on the sphinx. In Egyptian art a sphinx shows the king with the body of a lion, as a sign of his power. The Great Sphinx at Giza was carved from the bedrock in the 4th Dynasty, most likely under King Khafre, and was later seen as a form of Horus, Horemakhet.

Where the game departs from the myth

  • Apep diving under the sand, spitting venom orbs and summoning scarab swarms are the game's invention. In the myth he attacks the sun god's boat and is driven back each time.
  • In the game Apep swallows the sun and the arena goes dark. The sources link Apep with the darkness of eclipses, but in the myth the sun god rises again every day.
  • A "Sphinx Warden" that fires beams from its eyes is invented. In Egyptian art the sphinx is an image of the king with a lion's body.

This section describes the myth and the history themselves; the sections below are the game's interpretation. Sources are at the end of the page.

In the game: enemies

Each enemy teaches you something. At the end of the realm, the elite tests those lessons at their hardest.

  • Mummy

    Horde

    Slow but tough; closes in from all around in large groups. When it dies it leaves a pile of bandages that briefly slows.

    Teaches: crowd control

  • Scarab Swarm

    Fast

    Comes zigzagging in swarms of 6–10. Dies in one hit and gives off a small glint of gold as it dies.

    Teaches: quick reactions

  • Jackal Guard

    Attacker

    Pulls its spear back and glows for 0.8 s, then lunges in a straight line.

    Teaches: reading attack warnings

  • Elite

    Sphinx Warden

    Elite

    Strikes the ground to send out a ring of sand and fires beams from its eyes. The blue light in its cracks is its weak point (×2 damage).

    Teaches: weak points

The realm's kit

The realm's own weapon, armour set and myth power.

  • Realm weapon

    Khopesh

    Thrown, it comes back like a boomerang; hits on the way out and back.

  • Armour set

    Sun Gold

    2 pieces: +15% gold. 4 pieces: Sand burst on a critical hit.

  • Myth power

    Sun Shield

    No damage taken; enemies that touch you take half your sword damage per second.
