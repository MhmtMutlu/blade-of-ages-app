Realm of Greece: Labyrinth Island

The realm of Greece covers maps 31–40, and its map is called Labyrinth Island. The labyrinth walls shift from time to time. Enemies here: Skeleton Hoplite, Satyr Archer and Harpy. Elite: Cyclops. Boss: Medusa, the Gorgon.

Exhibit note

In myth and history

In Greek myth Medusa is one of the three Gorgons, daughters of the sea deities Phorcys and Ceto. Hesiod's Theogony places the Gorgons beyond the river Ocean, in the frontier land towards Night. Her sisters Stheno and Euryale were immortal; Medusa alone was mortal. When Perseus cut off her head, Chrysaor and the winged horse Pegasus sprang from her neck. According to the National Archaeological Museum in Athens, her gaze turned anyone who looked her in the face to stone. Perseus killed her with Athena's help and gave the head to the goddess, who placed it on her aegis and shield.

Later accounts add detail. In Ovid's Metamorphoses, Perseus tells how, on the way to Medusa's home, he passed figures of men and animals turned to stone by the sight of her face. In the same work Minerva (Athena) changes Medusa's hair into snakes to punish a crime committed in her temple. The realm's elite, the Cyclops, comes from another tradition. In Homer's Odyssey, Odysseus tricks the Cyclops Polyphemus by giving his name as "Noman" (Nobody), then blinds him by driving a heated stake into his eye.

Where the game departs from the myth

  • Medusa as "the guardian of the labyrinth island" is the game's invention. Hesiod places the Gorgons beyond the river Ocean.
  • Ovid mentions figures turned to stone, but statues that come back to life as Skeleton Hoplites are the game's invention.
  • In the game the gaze only slows the hero and does not kill. In the myth her gaze turns people to stone.
  • Snake hair that spits venom orbs and the summoning of harpies are the game's invention.

This section describes the myth and the history themselves; the sections below are the game's interpretation. Sources are at the end of the page.

In the game: enemies

Each enemy teaches you something. At the end of the realm, the elite tests those lessons at their hardest.

  • Skeleton Hoplite

    Shielded

    Turns slowly toward the hero (90°/s) and walks where it faces. Its shield blocks half of the hits from the front (±60°) and flashes when it blocks. Strike from the side or behind: a hero circling it gets past the shield.

    Teaches: positioning

  • Satyr Archer

    Ranged

    Keeps 190 px away and leaps back if you come closer than 140 px. Draws its bow for 0.5 s, then shoots a purple, parryable arrow (every 2.6 s, 300 px range). Parry your way forward to close in on a ranged enemy.

    Teaches: beating ranged enemies by parrying

  • Harpy

    Flying

    Takes off when you come close (it can't be hit in the air); its shadow grows for 0.8 s where the hero stands, then it dives there in an arc (1.5× its bite). After landing it stays on the ground for 0.8 s and takes ×1.5 damage. It flies over the labyrinth walls.

    Teaches: threats from the air

  • Elite

    Cyclops

    Elite

    Raises its club and waits 1 s (red ring, 120 px), then slams the ground and sends out a shock wave (heavy hit). Every 4.5 s. Its single eye is its weak point: it glows pale blue at regular intervals and takes ×2 damage while glowing.

    Teaches: positioning against area damage

The realm's kit

The realm's own weapon, armour set and myth power.

  • Realm weapon

    Xiphos and Spear

    A piercing spear thrust in the direction you move.

  • Armour set

    Aegean Bronze

    2 pieces: Damage taken −12%. 4 pieces: 30% of parried projectiles fly back at enemies.

  • Myth power

    Mirror Shield

    Every projectile counts as parryable and is reflected back (Perseus's polished shield).
