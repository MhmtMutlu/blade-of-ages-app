Realm of Hatti: Storm Mountains

The realm of Hatti covers maps 41–50, and its map is called Storm Mountains. Lightning strikes after a warning. Enemies here: Storm Spirit, Mountain Goat Rider and Rock Giant. Elite: Ullikummi. Boss: Illuyanka.

Exhibit note

In myth and history

Illuyanka is the serpent in the Hittite myth of the storm god's fight (CTH 321 in the Catalogue of Hittite Texts). The text simply calls it "the serpent" and does not describe its appearance. The myth is tied to the purulli festival and told by Kella, priest of the storm god of Nerik. In the first version the serpent defeats the storm god. The goddess Inara prepares a feast and invites it. The serpent comes up with its children, eats and drinks its fill, and does not go back down into its hole. Hupašiya, a mortal who has made a pact with Inara, ties it up with a rope, and the storm god kills it.

In the second version the serpent takes the storm god's heart and eyes. The god's son marries the serpent's daughter and asks her household for them; the god recovers, fights the serpent again and kills it, and his own son with it. The elite, Ullikummi, comes from the Song of Ullikummi, preserved in a Hittite version (CTH 345). The god Kumarbi fathers a stone child named Ullikummi to crush the city of Kummiya and strike down the storm god. It is set on Upelluri's right shoulder and grows in the water. Ea has the ancient saw that once cut heaven and earth apart brought out, so that Ullikummi can be cut away beneath its feet.

Where the game departs from the myth

  • The game calls Illuyanka a "horned dragon-serpent"; the myth text does not describe the serpent's appearance.
  • The feast trap follows the myth closely, but in the myth the feast is for the serpent and its children; Storm Spirits that heal it are the game's invention.
  • Ullikummi growing every 8 seconds and the blue vein at its ankle are the game's interpretation. In the myth the stone giant grows in the water, and Ea has the old saw brought out to cut it away beneath its feet.
  • Illuyanka and Ullikummi come from two separate texts (the Illuyanka myth and the Song of Ullikummi); the game brings them together in one realm.

This section describes the myth and the history themselves; the sections below are the game's interpretation. Sources are at the end of the page.

In the game: enemies

Each enemy teaches you something. At the end of the realm, the elite tests those lessons at their hardest.

  • Storm Spirit

    Area control

    Hovers in the air and calls down lightning on the ground below after a 1 s warning. Two spirits merge into a storm cloud.

    Teaches: reading ground warnings

  • Mountain Goat Rider

    Raider

    Leaps from the rocks in an arc and swings an axe on landing. It is defenseless in mid-air.

    Teaches: counter-attacking with timing

  • Rock Giant

    Tank

    Claps its hands and hurls a cone of rock shards in front of it.

    Teaches: staying out of the cone

  • Elite

    Ullikummi

    Elite / growing

    The diorite giant that grows out of the sea in the Hurrian-Hittite 'Song of Ullikummi'. It starts small and grows every 8 s (+15% size, +10% damage, at most 4 times; its damage pips rise to 5); while growing it sends out a red shock ring for 1 s. In the myth Ea cuts it from its feet with a copper knife: the pale blue vein at its ankle is the weak point (only from behind, ×2 damage; it turns slowly, so a hero who runs past ends up behind it).

    Teaches: prioritising a threat before it grows

Boss · map 50

Illuyanka

The horned dragon-serpent that fights the storm god in Hittite myth. In the myth Inara lays out a feast; the serpent gorges itself and can't get back into its hole, and the mortal Hupašiya ties it up with a rope.

The realm's kit

The realm's own weapon, armour set and myth power.

  • Realm weapon

    Hittite Iron Sword

    The game's first iron weapon: swings a heavy arc where you face; double damage to armored enemies, pierces bronze shields.

  • Armour set

    Storm Iron

    2 pieces: +10% sword damage. 4 pieces: Lightning on the 3 nearest enemies every 6 s.

  • Myth power

    Storm Call

    Lightning strikes the strongest enemies in turn; elites and bosses first.
