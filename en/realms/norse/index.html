Realm of Scandinavia: Frozen Fjord

The realm of Scandinavia covers maps 51–60, and its map is called Frozen Fjord. Ice ponds make the hero slide. Here you fight Raven Flock, Ice Wolf and Draugr; the elite is Frost Giant and the boss is Fenrir.

Exhibit note

In myth and history

Fenrir (the Fenris-Wolf) is a giant wolf in Norse myth, told most fully in Snorri Sturluson's Prose Edda. The gods raise him among them, but prophecies say he is fated to be their destruction, so they decide to bind him. He breaks the first fetter, Lædingr, and then a second, much stronger one called Drómi. Dwarves then make Gleipnir, a band as soft as silk made of six impossible things, such as the noise of a cat's footfall. Fenrir agrees to be bound only if a god puts a hand in his mouth as a pledge of good faith. Týr does so, and loses his hand when the band holds.

The gods pass the fetter's chain through a great rock and fix it deep in the earth. At Ragnarök all bonds break and Fenrir gets loose; he advances with his lower jaw against the earth and his upper jaw against the sky, and swallows Odin. Odin's son Vidar then kills him by tearing his jaws apart. The realm's elite draws on the giants of the same tradition. Snorri calls the first being, Ymir, and his descendants rime-giants (hrímþursar), and at Ragnarök the rime-giants join the attack on the gods.

Where the game departs from the myth

  • Fenrir chained to a stake in the arena echoes the chain fixed through a rock in the myth, but chain links snapping every few lunges are the game's invention. In the myth Gleipnir holds until Ragnarök.
  • The Moon Howl and the summoning of Ice Wolves are invented. In Snorri's account it is another wolf, not Fenrir, that seizes the moon at Ragnarök.
  • The Frost Giant's ice pillars and chest crystal are the game's invention.

This section describes the myth and the history themselves; the sections below are the game's interpretation. Sources are at the end of the page.

In the game: enemies

Each enemy teaches you something. At the end of the realm the elite tests it at its hardest.

  • Raven Flock

    Flying

    Dives at gold on the ground and snatches up to 3 coins; cut it down and the coins drop back, otherwise it flies off with them after 6 s. With no gold on the ground it dives at the hero.

    Teaches: taking back lost loot

  • Ice Wolf

    Pack

    Comes as a pack: circles the hero, then they all lunge together. Its bite slows the hero by 30% for 1.5 s.

    Teaches: not getting surrounded

  • Draugr

    Resilient

    Undying Viking warrior. When it falls it sinks into an ice shell and rises 2 s later with 40% of its health; if the shell is broken it never rises. Gold drops only on its final death.

    Teaches: finishing the job: a two-stage enemy

  • Elite

    Frost Giant

    Elite / cage

    One of the rime giants of Norse myth (hrímþursar). Every 8 s it stamps: red marks glow for 1 s, then 7 ice pillars rise in a half ring around the hero. The ring's only open side faces the giant. The pillars stand for 5 s and stop the hero and walking enemies; a sword breaks a pillar in 2 hits. The ice crystal in its chest glows at intervals (weak point, ×2).

    Teaches: opening your own way out before you are trapped

Boss · map 60

Fenrir

The giant wolf of Norse myth that had to be bound. First Læding, then Drómi were put on it, and it broke both; at Ragnarök it breaks free of every bond.

The realm's kit

The realm's own weapon, armour set and myth power.

  • Realm weapon

    Spinning Axe

    An axe flies from the hero in a widening spiral; it strikes each enemy it touches once per throw.

  • Armour set

    Northern Fur

    2 pieces: Slows and sliding on ice are halved; +10% damage to slowed enemies. 4 pieces: Sword hits slow enemies by 30% for 1.5 s (elite 15%).

  • Myth power

    Fimbulwinter

    Enemies on the field freeze and cannot attack; hits on the frozen deal ×1.5. When it ends, their ice shatters.
