Heroes

The 21 heroes of Blades of Ages, each from a different myth and land. Every hero has their own weapon, play style and power.

  • Kaya "The Bull"

    Hunter-chief of Göbekli Tepe · brawny warrior

    Homeland
    Hill of Standing Stones
    Play style
    Close-range tank · shock wave · high health
    “Stone never forgets. Neither do I.”

  • Nefertari

    Daughter of the Sun · Egyptian princess, strategist

    Homeland
    Hourglass Desert
    Play style
    Wide orbit · Sun Shield · loot bonus
    “Beauty is not a mask; it is a strategy.”

  • Muwatalli

    Charioteer of Hattusa · chosen of the Storm God

    Homeland
    Gate of the Storm God
    Play style
    Chariot charge · lightning strike · mid range
    “The gods bear witness; I keep the count.”

  • Antiope

    Amazon of Themiscyra · athletic, defiant

    Homeland
    Forests of Themiscyra
    Play style
    Speed and evasion · twin short swords · crits
    “Catch me first, then we'll talk.”

  • Aristos

    Hoplite of Olympus's Threshold · honourable, stubborn

    Homeland
    Threshold of Olympus
    Play style
    Phalanx shield · knockback · defensive
    “With the shield, or on it.”

  • Nergal

    Keeper of the Gate of Babylon · warden of the underworld

    Homeland
    Gate of Babylon
    Play style
    Flame whip · life steal · heals through damage
    “Even the dead deserve to be remembered.”

  • Sigrún

    Valkyrie of the Rime Fjord · solemn

    Homeland
    Rime Fjord
    Play style
    Dive · wing shield · soul gathering
    “Speak your name. I will carry it.”

  • Brigid

    Druid of the Misty Stone Circle · healer, sage

    Homeland
    Misty Stone Circle
    Play style
    Healing field · green oath · slows the horde
    “Every leaf remembers a name.”

  • Master Jin

    Wandering Master of the Cloud Temple · calm

    Homeland
    Cloud Temple
    Play style
    Parry master · 3× parry damage
    “The sword does not turn; the world turns around it.”

  • Tomoe

    Onna-bugeisha of the Cherry Temple · graceful, sharp

    Homeland
    Cherry Temple
    Play style
    Wide naginata sweep · counter · long reach
    “The blossom falls; the blade remembers.”

  • Itzel

    Jaguar Warrior of the Feathered Serpent's Pyramid · predator

    Homeland
    Pyramid of the Feathered Serpent
    Play style
    Pouncing claws · night stealth · bleeding
    “The sun sets, and I rise.”

  • Tomyris

    Queen of the Steppe · wolf daughter of Tengri

    Homeland
    Steppe of Gök Tengri
    Play style
    Mounted speed · wolf summoning · arrow rain
    “The sky sees, the steppe remembers.”

  • Rostam

    Champion of Zabulistan · under the Simurgh's wing

    Homeland
    Mountain of the Simurgh
    Play style
    Champion's mace · seven-trials combo · overwhelming power
    “I passed seven gates; you are the eighth.”

  • Arjun

    Archer of Kurukshetra · wielder of Gandiva

    Homeland
    Plain of Kurukshetra
    Play style
    Long-range arrows · time-slowing aim · multiple targets
    “I don't see the target, only the bird's eye.”

  • Vasilisa

    Wise One of the Birch Forest · escaped from Baba Yaga

    Homeland
    Birch Forest
    Play style
    Fire-skull lantern · guiding doll (auto-target) · burning
    “Darkness is for those without a lamp.”

  • Oya

    Lady of the Storm · wind daughter of the Niger

    Homeland
    Niger Storm Coast
    Play style
    Whirlwind orbit · flings enemies · speed boost
    “The wind doesn't ask; it passes.”

  • Tāne

    Forest Warrior of Aotearoa · haka master

    Homeland
    Isle of Greenstone
    Play style
    Haka power (area buff) · jade mere strike · defense
    “My ancestors are with me; who is with you?”

  • Qori

    Priestess of the Temple of the Sun · golden daughter of the Andes

    Homeland
    Temple of the Sun
    Play style
    Sun mark (damage boost) · quipu chain · binding
    “Every knot is a name.”

  • Lucius

    Centurion of the Legion · Rome's iron discipline

    Homeland
    Legion Camp
    Play style
    Testudo shield · pilum throw · battle line
    “The line does not break; we do.”

  • Yushin

    Young Master of the Hwarang · flower knight of Silla

    Homeland
    Hwarang Mountain
    Play style
    Flower-sword combo · charged power strike · agility
    “A flower lasts a season; honour lasts forever.”

  • Oblivion

    Homeland
    The Broken Clock

    This hero's story is still being written.
