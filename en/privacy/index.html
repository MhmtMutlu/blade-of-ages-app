This policy explains how your personal data is processed when you use Blade of Ages: Çağların Kılıcı, the mobile game for iPhone and iPad, and this website (the “Service”).

Data controller: Mehmet Mutlu, an individual developer living in Turkey (“we”). Contact: destek@mm-apps.com. The rules for using the game are in the Terms of Use. This policy is provided for information and does not require your consent. The Turkish version is the original; this is a translation.

Summary

  • We do not ask for your identity. You do not need to give a name, email address, phone number, postal address or date of birth to play. We do not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Players are recorded under random IDs. On the daily trial and weekly league rankings you can appear, if you wish, only under a nickname the game generates. On first launch a random account is created for you. This ID does not contain your name, but legally it still counts as personal data (pseudonymous data), and this policy covers it.
  • We process only what the game needs: your progress, your battle results and the battle recordings used to verify them, your in-game currency and items, and your purchases.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We use no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. This version of the game has no ads.
  • Our server is in Turkey. Data leaves Turkey only when you open the diamond store and make a purchase (Service providers).
  • You can delete your account inside the game (Account deletion).

3. What other players see

Only two rankings:

  • The daily trial ranking. Each day the 10 players with the highest scores are shown to all players with their ranks and scores.
  • The weekly league group. Up to 30 players who are in the same group in the same week of the league see each other's places and Glory points for that week. The server forms the group; it cannot be changed.

The same choice applies to both rankings. You choose how you appear in the ranking: under the nickname the game gives you, or as “Unnamed traveller”. Until you choose, you appear as Unnamed traveller, and you can change your choice at any time. You do not type the nickname yourself; the server generates it from a word list, and your real name and account ID are never shown. Outside the ranking, your own rank and the number of players who took part that day are shown only to you. The game has no chat and no friend list.

4. Service providers and international transfers

  • Güzel Hosting (GNET İnternet Telekomünikasyon A.Ş., Turkey): the virtual server service on which our server and backups are hosted. The database and authentication run on it with software we operate. Backups are encrypted on the server and kept for 30 days in Güzel Hosting's backup space in Turkey.
  • RevenueCat Inc. (USA): verification of in-app purchases. Only when you open the diamond store and make a purchase, RevenueCat receives your random account ID, your purchase details and the device information its software collects (device model, iOS version, language, App Store country and the device identifier Apple gives this app). If you never open the store, no data is sent to RevenueCat. RevenueCat processes this on our behalf under its published terms of service. Privacy
  • Apple Inc.: the App Store, in-app payments and refunds; an independent controller under its own policy. Privacy
  • Email: our support address is hosted by Güzel Hosting's business email service in Turkey. The replies we send are delivered through Güzel Hosting's mail relay service, MailChannels Corporation (Canada); the reply and your email address pass through its servers in transit.
  • The providers of this website are listed under This website.

International transfers: our server is in Turkey. Data sent to RevenueCat (USA) when you open the diamond store and make a purchase, and data sent to Apple when you use the App Store, is transferred outside Turkey. Data sent to Apple is processed as part of your choice to use the App Store and your own relationship with Apple. Data may be disclosed to competent public authorities where the law requires it.

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: the controller and our server are in Turkey, and your data is processed and stored there. There is no EU adequacy decision for Turkey. The retention periods, security measures and rights in this policy apply to you as well.

5. Ads and tracking

This version of the game has no ads and no advertising software; we do not ask for iOS tracking permission and we do not use the IDFA. If optional rewarded ads are added later, we will update this policy before that version is released.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your account and game data for as long as the account exists.
  • The input part of a battle recording is deleted 30 days after the result is issued; the result itself (map, reward, time) stays with your account. A result that cannot be verified within 30 days is dropped.
  • Daily trial records (ticket, score, ranking row) are deleted after 35 days; the reward already granted stays on your account.
  • Weekly league records (week, tier, group, Glory, place) are deleted after 12 weeks.
  • Cheating and inconsistency flags are kept for up to 12 months.
  • When you delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete account), your account, sign-in sessions and the game data tied to it are deleted immediately, and they are removed from the encrypted backups within 30 days. Exceptions, with the link to your account ID removed: diamond transactions and cheating flags are kept for up to 12 months, and purchase and refund records (product, transaction ID, date) are kept as the record of the sale and the refund. We keep a record of the deletion itself for 24 months, with only a date and a random request number and without your ID. Details: Account deletion.
  • Your customer record at RevenueCat is not deleted automatically when you delete your account; write to us if you want it deleted and we will have RevenueCat delete it.
  • Our server's technical logs (request time, error details) are limited in size; older lines are deleted automatically.
  • After a request is resolved, support emails are kept for as long as they are needed as evidence, then deleted.
  • Deleting the app from your device only deletes the data on the device; to delete the account on the server, use “Delete account” in the game first.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and backups are encrypted. Sensitive calculations such as rewards, balances and prices happen only on the server; only one service on the server can access game data, and only the developer administers the server. No system is completely secure; if we become aware of a data breach, we will take steps to limit its impact and make the notifications required by law.

8. Children

Blade of Ages is not designed specifically for children and is not in the App Store's Kids category. We do not ask for a name, email or date of birth. If we learn that a child under 13 (or a higher age if one applies in your country) has given us personal data, we will delete the account. Parents can write to destek@mm-apps.com for this.

9. This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics tools, external fonts or embedded content, and it makes no requests to other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA) and Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) provides the domain's DNS; the site's traffic does not pass through Cloudflare. GitHub processes your visit's IP address and browser information under its own policy to deliver and protect the site. GitHub privacy · Cloudflare privacy

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Turkey's KVKK (Art. 11) and other applicable law, you have the right to know whether your data is processed, to request information and a copy, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict processing and object to it, and to receive your data in a portable format.

How to apply: you can delete your account inside the game right away. For other requests, write to destek@mm-apps.com. Because we keep no name or email, we can match your account only with information from your device, and we may ask for more information to confirm that the request comes from you. We respond free of charge within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with Turkey's Personal Data Protection Board or with the data protection authority in your country.

11. Changes

When we add a new data flow to the game (for example rewarded ads or Sign in with Apple), we update this policy before that version is released. The current version is always on this page. This policy does not limit your statutory rights.
