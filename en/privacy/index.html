This policy explains how your personal data is processed when you use Blades of Ages (Turkish title Çağların Kılıcı), the mobile game for iPhone and iPad, and this website (the “Service”).

Controller: Mehmet Mutlu, an individual developer living in Turkey (“we”). Contact: destek@mm-apps.com. The rules for using the game are in the Terms of Use. This policy is for your information and does not ask for your consent. The Turkish version is the original; this is a translation.

Summary

  • We do not ask who you are. You do not need to give a name, email address, phone number, postal address or date of birth to play. We do not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Players are stored under random IDs. On first launch a random account is created for you. The ID does not contain your name, but legally it still counts as personal data (pseudonymous data), and this policy covers it.
  • We process only what the game needs: your progress, your battle results and the battle recordings used to verify them, your in-game currency and items, and your purchases.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We use no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. This version of the game has no ads.
  • Our server is in Turkey. Data leaves Turkey only when you open the diamond store and make a purchase (Service providers).
  • You can delete your account inside the game (Account deletion).

3. What other players see

Nothing. The game has no chat, friend list or player names. In the daily trial only you see your own place and how many players took part that day; your name, ID and result are not shown to other players.

4. Service providers and international transfers

  • Güzel Hosting (GNET İnternet Telekomünikasyon A.Ş., Turkey): the virtual server that hosts our server and backups. The database and authentication run on it with software we operate. Backups are encrypted on the server and kept for 30 days in Güzel Hosting's backup space in Turkey.
  • RevenueCat Inc. (USA): verification of in-app purchases. Only when you open the diamond store and make a purchase, RevenueCat receives your random account ID, your purchase details and the device information its software collects (device model, iOS version, language, App Store country and the device identifier Apple gives this app). If you never open the store, nothing is sent to RevenueCat. RevenueCat processes this on our behalf under its published terms of service. Privacy
  • Apple Inc.: the App Store, in-app payments and refunds; an independent controller under its own policy. Privacy
  • Email: our support address is hosted on Güzel Hosting's business email service in Turkey. Our replies are delivered through Güzel Hosting's mail relay, MailChannels Corporation (Canada); the reply and your email address pass through its servers in transit.
  • The providers of this website are listed under This website.

International transfers: our server is in Turkey. Data sent to RevenueCat (USA) when you open the diamond store and buy, and to Apple when you use the App Store, leaves Turkey. Data sent to Apple is processed as part of your choice to use the App Store and your own relationship with Apple. Data may be disclosed to competent public authorities where the law requires it.

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: the controller and our server are in Turkey, and your data is processed and stored there. There is no EU adequacy decision for Turkey. The retention periods, security measures and rights in this policy apply to you as well.

5. Ads and tracking

This version of the game has no ads and no advertising software; we do not ask for iOS tracking permission and we do not use the IDFA. If optional rewarded ads are added later, we will update this policy before that version is released.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your account and game data for as long as the account exists.
  • The input part of a battle recording is deleted 30 days after the result is decided; the result itself (map, reward, time) stays with your account. A result that cannot be verified within 30 days is dropped.
  • Cheating and inconsistency flags are kept for up to 12 months.
  • When you delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete account), your account, sign-in sessions and the game data tied to it are deleted immediately, and they leave the encrypted backups within 30 days. Exceptions, with the link to your account removed: diamond movements and cheating flags for up to 12 months; purchase and refund records (product, transaction ID, date) as the record of the sale and the refund. We record the deletion itself only with a date and a random request number, without your ID, for 24 months. Details: Account deletion.
  • Your customer record at RevenueCat is not deleted automatically when you delete your account; write to us if you want it deleted and we will have RevenueCat delete it.
  • Our server's technical logs (request time, error details) are limited in size; old lines are removed automatically.
  • Support emails are kept for as long as needed as proof after the request is closed, then deleted.
  • Deleting the app from your device only deletes the data on the device; to delete the account on the server, use “Delete account” in the game first.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and backups are encrypted. Sensitive calculations such as rewards, balances and prices happen only on the server; a single service on the server can reach game data, and only the developer administers the server. No system is completely secure; if we learn of a data breach we will act to limit its impact and make the notifications the law requires.

8. Children

Blades of Ages is not designed specifically for children and is not in the App Store's Kids category. We do not ask for a name, email or date of birth. If we learn that a child under 13 (or a higher age where that applies in your country) has given us personal data, we will delete the account. Parents can write to destek@mm-apps.com for this.

9. This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics tools, external fonts or embedded content, and it makes no requests to other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA) and the domain is served through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). These providers process your visit's IP address and browser information under their own policies to deliver and protect the site. GitHub privacy · Cloudflare privacy

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Turkey's KVKK (Art. 11) and other applicable law, you have the right to know whether your data is processed, to ask for information and a copy, to correction, to erasure, to restrict and object to processing, and to receive your data in a portable format.

How to apply: you can delete your account inside the game right away. For other requests, write to destek@mm-apps.com. Because we keep no name or email, we can match your account only with information from your device, and we may ask for more information to confirm that the request comes from you. We answer free of charge within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority or to Turkey's Personal Data Protection Board.

11. Changes

When we add a new data flow to the game (for example rewarded ads or Sign in with Apple), we update this policy before that version is released. The current version is always on this page. This policy does not limit your statutory rights.
