About the game

Blades of Ages is a one-thumb action game in which outward-facing blades spin around your hero. Cut through waves of monsters and defeat bosses across 5 ages and 12 mythological realms.

5 ages, 12 mythological realms, 120 maps. Every realm has its own enemies, map rule, weapon and boss. When an age's realms are cleared, its guardian waits at the gate.

  • Played with one thumb: the blades attack on their own while you walk or stand. Walking widens their orbit; standing still draws it in and speeds it up.
  • Every realm has its own enemies, map rule, map structures, weapon, armour set and a three-phase boss.
  • A journey that starts with the myths of Göbekli Tepe, Sumer, Egypt, Greece, the Hittites and Scandinavia, with the myths of Anatolia at the front.
  • Free to download; no forced ads, and nothing with random contents is sold for real money.
