Story: the Tree of Ages and Silik

Once there was, and once there was not.

Blade of Ages: Çağların Kılıcı is an action game in which the world's tales live on the branches of a single tree, the Tree of Ages. Each branch is an age, each cluster of leaves a mythological realm: the standing stones of Göbeklitepe, the cedar forest of Sumer, the sands of Egypt, the labyrinth island of Greece, the storm mountains of the Hittites, the frozen fjords of the North and more. You are a Rememberer who tells these tales again; the blades spinning around you are your memory.

The Tree of Ages and the Spring of Memory

The Storyteller says every people knows this tree by its own name. At its roots lies the Spring of Memory. Every tale that is told seeps into it as a single drop and feeds the tree.

Silik

A tale that is not told fades. For a while now the tree has been fading: the monsters of the tales have lost their way and spilled over their banks. Göbeklitepe's stone boars roam the Egyptian sands; harpies circle the Hittite peaks. People gave this fading a name: Silik.

Rememberers and the Memory Blades

The tree wakes those who can still tell the tales. The blades around you are the memories of other heroes. When you walk, the orbit widens, because memory wants to spread. When you stop, the blades gather and spin faster, because remembering asks you to stand still. Every creature you defeat returns to its own tale, and the light it leaves behind becomes gold. Forgotten, it turns to dust. Remembered, it turns to gold.

The Storyteller

A voice whose face you never see. It is the one who tells the tales of the ages.

The ages

First Age · Ancient Age · North and Steppe. At the gate of each age waits a guardian in which that age's tales have tangled together.

Realms

Unutuş

A traveller from the Broken Clock who does not know their own name. Their story is still being written.

Inspired by myth, made for the game

The Tree of Ages is this game's own tale. It is inspired by real myths and does not replace them. Gods of living faiths are never enemies; the bosses are the monsters and villains of the myths themselves. Each realm page explains the myth and where the game departs from it.
