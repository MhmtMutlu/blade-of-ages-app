Playable demo: blades spinning around the hero cut down Göbekli Tepe monsters coming from four directions; defeated monsters turn into gold.

iPhone and iPad · one thumb

The blades spin. You walk.

Blades of Ages is a one-thumb action game in which outward-facing blades spin around your hero. Cut through waves of monsters and defeat bosses across 5 ages and 12 mythological realms.

Coming soon to the App Store

One thumb, two orbits

No extra buttons. The blades attack on their own; you choose where to stand and when to walk.

  • Walk

    The blades spin in a wide orbit and clear your path as you move through the crowd.

  • Stand

    The blades draw in close and spin faster, melting a tough enemy at short range.

  • Parry

    Purple projectiles vanish when they hit a blade. Red area warnings have to be dodged.

Journey through the Ages

5 ages, 12 mythological realms, 120 maps. Every realm has its own enemies, map rule, weapon and boss. When an age's realms are cleared, its guardian waits at the gate.

Fair play

  • No forced ads

    An ad plays only when you choose to watch one, and it gives you a reward.

  • Respect for myths

    You never fight the gods of living faiths. Bosses are the myths' own monsters and villains.

  • No gore

    Defeated enemies crumble into their materials and turn into gold.

Frequently asked questions

When does Blades of Ages come out?

Soon, on the App Store. The release date will appear on this page once it is set.

Which devices can I play on?

iPhone and iPad.

Is it free?

It is free to download. The game has optional in-app purchases and no forced ads.

Which languages is it available in?

Turkish, English, Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese, German, French.

Are the myths in the game real?

The realms draw on real mythologies: Göbekli Tepe, the Epic of Gilgamesh, and Egyptian, Greek and Hittite myths. The bosses are monsters from these myths; the game's story and some characters are its own.
